Support

Hatch is a pencil sketchbook for iPad, made by one person. If something is broken or confusing, write to me and I will answer.

Email: hatchsketchbook@gmail.com

Before you write

Nothing happens when I draw. Hatch draws with Apple Pencil. Fingers move, zoom and turn the page; they don't draw.

How do I change the pencil's size or darkness? Use the strip on the left edge: the top line is size, the second is the grade (softer and darker towards the top).

Where are my other pages? Tap the house at the top left.

I deleted a page by mistake. Open the sketchbook and look under "Recently deleted". Pages stay there for 30 days.

I want an older version of a page back. In the sketchbook, hold the page and choose "Earlier versions".

How do I export? Tap the gear at the top right. You can export the page, part of it, a timelapse, or the whole sketchbook as a PDF.

My exports look small. Turn off Performance mode in Settings before exporting. Performance mode keeps the page at screen sharpness to save battery.

The free week and the unlock

What happens after the free week? Drawing waits for the unlock: $2.99, once. Everything you have drawn still opens and exports.

I bought the unlock and it's asking again. Tap the gear, then the Hatch row, then "Restore purchase". Make sure the iPad is signed in to the Apple Account you bought it with.

I want a refund. Purchases are handled by Apple. Ask at reportaproblem.apple.com.

When you write, it helps to include

  • Your iPad model and iPadOS version
  • What you did just before the problem
  • A screenshot or screen recording, if you can
