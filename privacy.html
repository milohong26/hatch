Privacy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Hatch is a sketchbook for iPad. It is made by one person.

The short version

Hatch does not collect anything about you. It has no account, no ads and no analytics. The only time it uses the internet is to ask Apple's App Store about the free week and the unlock.

Your drawings

Everything you draw is stored on your iPad, inside the app. It is not uploaded anywhere. If you back up your iPad with iCloud or a computer, your drawings are part of that backup, under Apple's terms, not mine.

A drawing leaves the app only when you choose to share or export it, through the iPad's own share sheet.

Photos

If you choose "Save Image" or "Save Video" when exporting, Hatch asks for permission to add that file to your photo library. It can only add; it never reads your photos.

Purchases

The free week and the unlock are handled by Apple. Hatch asks the App Store on your iPad whether your Apple Account has them, and that is all it learns. I never see your name, card or Apple Account.

Beta testers

If you use a TestFlight build, Apple shares crash reports and the feedback you choose to send, as described in TestFlight's own terms.

Children

Hatch has no content or features aimed at collecting anything from anyone, of any age.

Changes

If this ever changes, this page will say so before the app does anything different.
